# Best Open-Source LLMs for Laptop

### Summary
The best open-source large language models (LLMs) to run on a laptop, according to various sources, are:

1. **Qwen 3.8** - Known for its high performance and quality, making it a top choice for many use cases.
2. **DeepSeek V4** - Another high-performing model that has gained popularity.
3. **Mistral** - Known for its permissive licensing, which makes it an attractive option for those looking for flexibility.
4. **Gemma 4** - Recommended for running on a laptop due to its optimized size and performance.

### Key Findings
- **Qwen 3.8** and **DeepSeek V4** are highly ranked for general use cases.
- **Mistral** is noted for its permissive licensing, making it suitable for projects that require flexible licensing terms.
- **Gemma 4** is highlighted for its suitability for running on laptops due to its optimized size and performance.
